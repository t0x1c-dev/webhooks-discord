About

Who even is this.

Proton Services is a free set of Discord message builders. Components V2 and classic embeds. No account, no install, nothing uploaded to us.

You build in the browser, preview like Discord, export JSON, or send straight through your own webhook. We don’t sit in the middle of that.

Made for people who ship bot messages, status posts, and clean layouts without fighting the API by hand every time.
